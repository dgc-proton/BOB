Colour coding:
The bundle of four cables go diagonally across, so the ones sticking out the right are for the sensors on the left and vice-versa.
Red: 5V (apart from single wire from battery to H-Bridge which is 12V)
Black: ground (-ve battery terminal)
Yellow (bundle of four cables): object sensors echo
Blue, bundle of 4: object sensors trigger
Green, bundle of 4: line sensors Vcc
White, bundle of 4: line sensors out
Bundle of 6, one of each colour: these are from the H-Bridge to the MCU so are fully visible

===================================================================================
FIXES REQUIRED
Common Pins:
GND H-Bridge: Ultrasonic Grounds, Line Sensor Grounds, Arduino Ground
5V H-Bridge: Ultrasonic Power, ESP32 VIN
Fixes:
Pin Soldering on Ultrasonics, Add ESP32 VIN to crimped connection

Battery Pins:
1. Power --> +12
2. Ground --> GND

H-Bridge
ENA --> 13
In1 --> 12
In2 --> 14

ENB --> 15
In3 --> 2
In4 --> 4

Line Sensors
Left Power --> 26
Left Out --> 27
Right Power --> 33
Right Out --> 25

Obj Sensors
Left Trigger --> 16
Left Echo --> 17
Right Trigger --> 5
Right Echo --> 18
